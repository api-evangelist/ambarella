# Ambarella

> Ambarella is a fabless semiconductor company (NASDAQ: AMBA) that designs low-power system-on-chip (SoC) processors for edge AI and computer vision. Its CVflow architecture powers intelligent cameras, automotive ADAS/autonomous driving, robotics, security, and industrial edge-AI devices. Ambarella does not expose a public HTTP/REST API; developers work through the Cooper Developer Platform, the CVflow SDK/toolchain, the Model Garden on HuggingFace, and a published set of on-device Agent Skills.
